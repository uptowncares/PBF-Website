Our Programs

At the Peggy Beatrice Foundation, we are dedicated to bringing hope and restoration to communities in need, one family at a time. We offer a range of programs that aim to meet the physical, spiritual, and emotional needs of those who are struggling, including the homeless, underserved, and marginalized

Serve a Soul

Tuesday Feeding

Our Tuesday Feeding program is dedicated to providing nourishing meals to the homeless in the heart of Skid Row. We believe that by meeting practical needs and empowering and educating the underserved in wholeness and health, we can help them take the first steps towards a better future.

Clothes, Hygiene Kits, Encouragement

We also provide those we serve with clothes, hygiene kits, and encouragement, as we believe in the importance of a healthy mind, body, and spirit. We believe that by addressing basic needs and offering support and encouragement, we can help those in need take the first steps towards their truest desires.

A Creative Wonderland

We are dedicated to fostering creativity and imagination in children through our ‘A Creative Wonderland’ program. We believe that every child has the potential to create something special and unique, and we are committed to providing the resources and support they need to bring their artistic visions to life.
Through ‘A Creative Wonderland,’ we provide kids with the tools and resources they need to explore their creativity and express themselves through various crafts and artistic mediums. We believe that by giving children the support they need to pursue their passions, we can help them develop a love for art and creativity from a young age
