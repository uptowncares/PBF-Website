The Mission

At the Peggy Beatrice Foundation, we are committed to promoting healthy family structures and bringing hope and restoration to communities in need, one family at a time. We believe that by providing the necessary support and tools, we can empower individuals and families to reach their full potential and create a brighter future for all. But we can’t do it alone. We need your help to continue our important work. With your generous donation, you can be a part of creating positive change in the lives of families and communities. Together, we can make a lasting impact and bring hope and restoration to those in need. Thank you for considering a donation to the Peggy Beatrice Foundation. Your support is greatly appreciated.

The Vision

At the Peggy Beatrice Foundation, we envision a world where the underserved are embraced, supported, and restored through resources and outreach. We believe that by starting at the family level and working our way to entire communities, we can create lasting positive change and empower those in need to reach their full potential. We are dedicated to bringing hope and restoration to those who have been marginalized or overlooked, and we are committed to creating a brighter future for all. We invite you to join us in this important work and be a part of creating positive change in the lives of families and communities.

About the Founder

Meet Pierre A. Johnson, the CEO of Touch Network and a dedicated advocate for strengthening families and communities. Pierre has been at the forefront of the Peggy Beatrice Foundation’s mission to bring hope and restoration to those in need, and Pierre is passionate about empowering individuals and families to reach their full potential. With a strong background in the non-for-profit industry, Pierre has a unique perspective on the challenges facing families today and is committed to finding innovative solutions to address them. Pierre is excited to continue his work with the Peggy Beatrice Foundation and make a positive impact in the lives of those we serve.
