About the Founder

Meet Pierre A. Johnson, the CEO of Touch Network and a dedicated advocate for strengthening families and communities. Pierre has been at the forefront of the Peggy Beatrice Foundation’s mission to bring hope and restoration to those in need, and Pierre is passionate about empowering individuals and families to reach their full potential. With a strong background in the non-for-profit industry, Pierre has a unique perspective on the challenges facing families today and is committed to finding innovative solutions to address them. Pierre is excited to continue his work with the Peggy Beatrice Foundation and make a positive impact in the lives of those we serve.
